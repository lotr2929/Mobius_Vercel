Your profile

Who you are, as Mobius should know you. This text goes to the AI with every message, so write only what you are happy for the AI providers to see. Mobius updates it weekly from your conversations and uses the update straight away; your earlier version is kept below. If you edit it, Mobius leaves it alone for a day.

Linked folders

Link folders from Google Drive so Mobius can read your documents and help with your work. Your files stay where they are; Mobius copies only the text. It reads gradually, newest files first, up to the limit you set for each folder. It understands PDF, Word, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON, Google Docs and Google Sheets, and skips anything over 25 MB.

OneDrive, Dropbox, Box and other services: you can paste their links and Mobius will record them, but it cannot read them yet. Each needs its own one-off set-up. Anything read from a linked folder can be sent to the AI models when it is relevant, and the free tiers of some providers may use what they receive for training, so link only folders you are comfortable with.

Development notes

Your to-do list for Mobius itself: instructions, fixes and ideas to work through together at the laptop. Type them in from any device. They are kept in the cloud, are never sent to any AI model, and we go through the open ones at the start of the next session. Click an item's text to edit it; tick it when it is done.

Notes Mobius keeps

Things you asked it to remember, or definitions and preferences it noted from what you said. They go to the AI with every message. Click a note's text to edit it. In the chat you can also say "Remember that …", "Forget #14", "Show notes".

Suggestions waiting for you

Things Mobius noticed in your conversations that you may want remembered. Nothing is kept until you save it. Unreviewed suggestions lapse after two months.

Learning from your messages

What Mobius does when you clearly define a term of your own, correct it, or state a preference, without being told to remember.

AI models

Answers are tried on these in order. A model that fails is skipped for a while; the list is re-checked against what each provider offers every few hours.

Storage & housekeeping

Mobius keeps itself tidy: it rebuilds documents whose search data is missing, retires old suggestions, notes and logs, and checks how full the database is. Nothing is deleted outright; see Backup below.
